ABOUT

As a mobile game developer, I thrive on blending art and technology to create immersive gaming experiences. I’m passionate about learning and evolving, aiming to build games for every platform and genre.

I’ve contributed to several projects with 50 Mn+ downloads, collaborating with Funvent Studios (formerly known as Spearmint Games), an industry-leading mobile game studio with a collective 300 Mn+ downloads. My work involves harnessing the power of robust game engines like Unity and Axmol (Cocos2d-x), requiring strong expertise in languages such as C# and C++.
